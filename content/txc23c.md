## Quantum Meets Kubernetes: Deploying Serverless Quantum Workloads on OpenShift - Paul Schweigert & Michael "Max" Maximilien, IBM (IBM TechXchange 2023)

Quantum computing holds the promise of faster algorithms, novel solutions to intricate problems, and potentially shaking up cryptography. But what does it mean for the cloud-native landscape? Join us as we clear up the pivotal role Kubernetes will play as quantum computing transitions from the lab to mainstream usage. Our session will delve into various frameworks for managing and running quantum workloads using OpenShift and the open-source toolkit, Qiskit. We will also showcase how quantum serverless can become a significant facet of the future of cloud computing.
