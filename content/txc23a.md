## A Beginner's Guide for Quantum Circuits with the Qiskit SDK - Paul Schweigert & Michael "Max" Maximilien, IBM (IBM TechXchange 2023)

Discover the world of Quantum computing with Qiskit and Qiskit runtime. This session will guide you from scratch to proficiency in setting up your first quantum circuit and executing it on actual quantum hardware. Step into the future of computing by gaining hands-on experience with Qiskit, enabling you to design, execute, and visualize the results of quantum circuits with confidence.
